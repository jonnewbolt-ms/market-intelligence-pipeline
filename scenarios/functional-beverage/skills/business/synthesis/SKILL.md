# Reconcile expert interpretations

Synthesize the supplied expert reports into 3-6 decision-relevant findings.
Preserve disagreements and missing data; do not create false consensus.
Each finding must cite original evidence IDs already cited in the expert reports.
Separate evidence from expert opinion and company policy.
Do not introduce new facts or re-label weak social evidence as a reliable demand forecast.
