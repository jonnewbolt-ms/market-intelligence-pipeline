# Prepare a human decision, not an autonomous launch

Produce 2-4 distinct candidate pilots, including a standard-can option and, if relevant,
a specialty-bottle alternative. Every option must cite supplied synthesis finding IDs.
Use the packaging enum exactly. Explain subjective 1-5 consumer, commercial, and
feasibility scores in each rationale. Do not calculate or assert the final ranking:
Python applies policy weights and manufacturing gates.

Identify an audience, channel, and proposition in each option's name or rationale.
Define a bounded next action, measurable success and stop criteria, and the unknowns
that must be resolved first. Label proposed thresholds as experiment design choices,
not facts from the evidence. No spending, production, or health-claim authorization.
